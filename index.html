AI Voice Assistant

Not a chatbot — the main way people use WasteLoop

Request pickups, check EcoPoints, check rewards, track a collection, ask waste questions, report unsafe waste, or set reminders — all by talking, in your preferred language.

What you can ask it to do

  • ♻️Request pickup — describe your waste and it's scheduled.
  • 🪙Check EcoPoints — hear your current balance.
  • 🎁Check rewards — hear what you can redeem right now.
  • 🚚Track collection — get the status of a pending pickup.
  • ❓Ask waste questions — "where does plastic go?" and similar.
  • 🚨Report unsafe waste — start an anonymous WasteWatch report.
  • ⏰Set a reminder — get nudged before your next pickup.
